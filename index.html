About

Nutrition care that fits your life

I am Dt. Soujanya, a Clinical Nutritionist & Dietitian based in Hyderabad, with a Master's degree in Clinical Nutrition & Dietetics and over 3 years of professional experience. I have been in professional clinical practice as a Clinical Nutritionist & Dietitian since 2023.

My professional experience includes providing personalized nutrition and dietary guidance for individuals with diverse health and lifestyle goals — including weight management, PCOD, thyroid-related nutrition, and nutrition support in cancer, kidney, liver, and post-CABG care — within my professional scope and as part of a broader healthcare approach.

Effective nutrition care goes beyond simply giving a diet chart. It requires understanding a person's health needs, lifestyle, food preferences, routine, cultural background, and ability to follow recommendations consistently.

My goal is to make nutrition personalized, practical, evidence-informed, and sustainable.

My Approach

Six steps, one goal: habits that last

01

Listen

Every consultation begins by understanding you — lifestyle, food preferences, daily routine, eating patterns, health history, activity levels, goals, and practical challenges.

02

Assess

I evaluate existing dietary habits and identify areas where meaningful changes can be made.

03

Personalize

Instead of giving the same diet to everyone, I develop recommendations based on your nutritional needs and lifestyle.

04

Educate

You should understand why you are making a dietary change — knowledge makes change stick.

05

Support

Regular follow-ups help identify challenges, monitor progress, and make appropriate adjustments.

06

Sustain

The goal is to create habits that continue beyond the diet plan.

Initial Consultation→ Nutrition Assessment→ Personalized Plan→ Implementation→ Follow-up→ Sustainable Habits
A healthy diet is not about perfection. It is about making better choices consistently and creating habits that fit your life.
